# Kochi: Homecoming — visual reference brief

This is an art-direction brief for the procedural Android prototype. References guide atmosphere, landmark silhouettes and wayfinding; they are not copied game assets or a claim of a surveyed 1:1 Kochi map.

## Local streets and waterfront

- **Fort Kochi streets:** use the variety of plaster colours, shutters, balconies, verandahs and tiled-roof rhythms to break up repetitive box façades. Reference: https://backpackersunited.in/destinations/kochi-kerala/attraction/fort-kochi
- **Marine Drive / backwaters:** the promenade should read as a continuous pedestrian edge, with paving changes, rails, benches, lamps and planted areas. Reference: https://www.shiftcities.org/post/trees-cities-implementing-nature-based-solutions-india
- **Fort Kochi fishing-net silhouette:** large, recognisable timber-boom shapes give the waterfront a specifically local skyline rather than generic city props.
- **Port skyline:** container cranes and ferries should provide distant points of interest across the backwaters, not obstruct the playable road or the opening scene.

## Gameplay readability

Prioritise a strong silhouette and contrast between the walkable route, road, waterfront edge, storefronts and distant landmarks. Keep the opening return to Kochi grounded and quiet: no weapons or wanted level at the start. Improve materials and density gradually while protecting Android performance.

Level-design reference: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/gdc-2018-level-design-workshop-an-expert-roundtable-q-a

## Android branding

Use the supplied KOCHI HOMECOMING artwork for the app icon, including Android's legacy icon and adaptive icon layers. Godot Android export reference: https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/tutorials/export/exporting_for_android.html
