# Kochi: Homecoming — Story Mission Plan

## Canon constraints
- Vivek is 25 and returns to Kochi in 2026 after being away for several years.
- His father Abraham has died. The death initially appears to be a road accident.
- Rafi is 32, a mechanic, garage owner and fixer who knows part of Abraham's criminal world.
- Abraham started as a driver and later became involved in smuggling, while keeping that life separate from his family.
- Do not confirm a conspiracy or reveal the possible cut brake line at the start.
- No weapons and no wanted level for Vivek at the beginning.

## Prologue — The Return
Purpose: establish Vivek, Kochi, the loss and the atmosphere without exposing the central mystery.
1. Arrive in a rain-soaked Kochi street.
2. Player learns movement and camera control naturally.
3. Navigate to the family house.
4. Short ambient cutscene or dialogue: Vivek has returned after Abraham's death.
Completion: player reaches the house entrance.

## Mission 01 — The Old House
Purpose: let the player explore a personal space and feel the absence.
1. Enter the house.
2. Inspect the living room and family objects.
3. Inspect a photograph or ordinary personal item.
4. Leave the house when ready.
No hidden evidence is confirmed in this mission.

## Mission 02 — The Mechanic
Purpose: introduce Rafi as a person, not as an exposition machine.
1. Travel through a compact neighborhood route to Rafi's garage.
2. Approach Rafi and interact.
3. Dialogue establishes that he knew Abraham and has limited knowledge.
4. Rafi mentions the accident only as currently understood.
Do not have Rafi explain the entire smuggling network.

## Mission 03 — The Last Drive
Purpose: first grounded look at the reported accident.
1. Travel to the road where Abraham's car was found.
2. Inspect the visible crash location and surrounding environment.
3. Use restrained environmental storytelling, not a forced reveal.
4. End on a question or an unanswered detail, not a confirmed conspiracy.

## Future mission candidates (not yet implemented)
- A normal day in the neighborhood to build familiarity.
- A return visit to the garage after the player learns more.
- Gradually uncover Abraham's past through separate leads.
- Driving lessons / ordinary vehicle travel before high-risk sequences.
